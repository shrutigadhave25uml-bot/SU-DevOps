# 7. Simulated Scrum Ceremonies

Use these prompts during team activities. Replace the example notes with what your team actually discussed.

## Sprint Planning
- Which prioritized stories can the team complete in this sprint?
- What is the Sprint Goal?
- What dependencies or risks are present?
- What acceptance criteria define completion?

**Example Sprint Goal:** Deliver student record creation, search, update, and deletion.

## Daily Scrum
Each development team member briefly answers:
- What did I complete yesterday?
- What will I work on today?
- What blockers or challenges do I have?

## Sprint Review
- Demonstrate completed features to stakeholders.
- Compare the increment with acceptance criteria.
- Collect feedback and add follow-up items to the backlog.

## Sprint Retrospective
Discuss:
- What went well?
- What challenges occurred?
- What should the team change in the next sprint?

**Action-item template:** Improvement | Owner | Target sprint/date | Status.
