# 1. Agile Product Vision

## Product name
Student Record and Academic Management Platform (CampusFlow)

## Product vision
"Develop a secure and user-friendly Student Record and Academic Management Platform that enables educational institutions to efficiently manage student records, attendance, academic performance, course registration, and reporting through a scalable web-based application."

## Product objective
Provide one simple workspace where authorized education staff can organize student records and review course, attendance, and academic performance information.

## Primary users
- Administrator: maintains student and course records.
- Faculty member: records attendance and marks.
- Academic staff: reviews student and course reports.
- Student: a future role for viewing their own academic information.

## Expected business benefits
- Reduce duplicated manual records.
- Make student information easier to search and update.
- Improve visibility of attendance and marks.
- Support timely academic reporting.

## MVP scope
Included in this prototype: student CRUD, course catalogue, attendance entry, marks entry, basic grade calculation, dashboard summaries, and CSV export.

Future scope: secure login, role-based access, password reset, student self-service, notifications, backend database, and multi-user access.

## Example success criteria
- A user can add, search, update, and delete a student record.
- A user can add a course and associate a student with it.
- Attendance can be saved by date.
- Marks percentages and grade bands are calculated consistently.
- Reports can be exported as CSV.
- Main views remain usable on common desktop and mobile screen widths.

## Constraints and assumptions
- This is an educational mini-project and front-end prototype.
- No backend service or real authentication is configured.
- Browser localStorage is used for demo persistence.
- Sample records are fictional and must not be treated as real student data.
